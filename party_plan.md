# One Piece 13th Birthday — Idea Menu

**Nothing here is settled.** This is the full menu of everything brainstormed so
far, so you can pick the best 5–6 stations and an ending. A suggested lineup is
at the very bottom, but it's just one pick from the menu.

Ratings: **Fun** = how well it lands with 13-year-olds. **Effort** = prep + setup.
(★ low … ★★★ high)

---

## Part 1 — Framing devices (pick any; they run across all stations)

| Device | What it is | Fun | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wanted posters + bounties** | Each kid gets a poster on arrival (online generator, photo as they walk in). Bounties rise on a whiteboard after each station; end at Yonko billions. Determines turn order later. | ★★★ | ★ |
| **Road Poneglyphs** | 4 red pieces earned one per station; only together (and decoded) do they reveal the One Piece's location. 3D-printed jigsaw version already designed (`openscad/`). | ★★★ | ★★ (printing) |
| **Devil Fruit powers** | Found early; one-time powers cashed in later (`printables/power_cards.html`). | ★★★ | ★ |
| **Berry coins** | Physical currency handed out per station instead of a whiteboard; dump the pile in the chest at the end. 3D-printable. | ★★ | ★★ |
| **Log Pose** | Compass on a wrist cradle that "points to the next island". Also a real navigation station. | ★★ | ★ (phone) / ★★ (printed) |
| **Timeskip** | Food break mid-party = "two years pass". Everyone returns with Haki wristbands = a bonus power for the second half. | ★★ | ★ |
| **Crews / teams** | Default is one cooperative crew. Option: split into Straw Hats vs Heart Pirates vs Kid Pirates ("Worst Generation race") for the competitive stations only. | ★★ | ★ |
| **Den Den Mushi calls** | Walkie-talkie in a printed snail shell; the "Marines" or "Kaido" call in threats between stations. | ★★★ | ★★★ |
| **Vivre Card** | A paper scrap that "points" to a person — the host palms it toward the next station. | ★ | ★ |

## Part 2 — Story beats and every game idea for each (in story order)

### East Blue

**Romance Dawn** — Luffy eats the Gum-Gum Fruit; Shanks gives him the straw hat.
- **Devil Fruit hunt** — hidden fruits (3D-printed hollow Gum-Gum fruits, or Sharpie-swirled apples), each with a power card inside. One per kid. Hand out straw hats. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Shanks' hat** — birthday kid formally receives "the hat" to open the party. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Shells Town (Zoro)** — tied to a post; Luffy fetches his swords.
- **Zoro's three swords** — pool-noodle sword held in the mouth, knock a cup off a post / cut a paper "steel" ribbon. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Rescue Zoro** — untie a knotted rope challenge. Fun ★ Effort ★

**Orange Town (Buggy)** — Chop-Chop Fruit, cannon.
- **Buggy piñata** — a round piñata as Buggy's head; he "splits into pieces" when hit. Fill with power cards + candy (merges with the Devil Fruit hunt). Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Buggy Ball dodge** — dodgeball with one soft ball = the Buggy cannon. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Syrup Village (Usopp)** — the liar/sniper.
- **Usopp's sniper** — slingshot marshmallows / Nerf at printed Marine targets, hit N. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Usopp's lie** — kids invent the wildest 30-second lie about their bounty; best one wins a power. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Baratie (Sanji)** — the floating restaurant.
- **Sanji's kitchen** — blindfolded taste test, name 5 foods. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Food fight / plating** — decorate a cupcake as a Straw Hat crew member. Fun ★★ Effort ★★

**Arlong Park (Nami)** — freeing Cocoyashi village.
- **Water challenge** — carry water in cups across the yard to fill Nami's "tangerine grove"; sponge relay. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Nami's map** — torn map pieces reassembled to show the next station. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Break the wall** — Luffy smashes Arlong Park: kick down a cardboard-box wall. Fun ★★★ Effort ★★

**Loguetown** — Roger's execution platform; the crew nearly caught by Smoker.
- **Escape Smoker** — a 60-second dash past a "smoke" adult (fog machine optional). Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Roger's last words** — host reads Roger's speech to launch the Grand Line half. Fun ★ Effort ★

### Grand Line — Paradise

**Reverse Mountain / Laboon** — compasses fail; the giant whale.
- **Log Pose navigation** — compass bearings and paces to the next station. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Sea King piñata** — a piñata "attacks" the ship entering the Grand Line; the Log Pose clue is inside. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Laboon** — throw beanbags into a whale's mouth (cardboard). Fun ★ Effort ★★

**Whiskey Peak / Little Garden** — bounty hunters; giants' duel.
- **Giants' duel** — pool-noodle sword duel, first to 3 touches. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Dinosaur egg carry** — egg-and-spoon across the yard. Fun ★ Effort ★

**Drum Island (Chopper)** — the doctor joins.
- **Chopper's medicine** — mix the right "cure" from labeled bottles (color-mixing puzzle) or a first-aid speed challenge. Fun ★★ Effort ★★
- **Snow** — if winter: snowball fort. Fun ★★★ Effort ★

**Alabasta (Crocodile / Baroque Works / Vivi)** — the sand kingdom.
- **Crocodile's cage** — dig 20 keys out of a sand bucket, one opens the padlock. Spray bottle = the sand fights back. 3D-printable decoy keys. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Baroque Works agents** — kids draw secret "Mr. 1 / Miss Doublefinger" cards; find your partner by codeword. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Vivi's X mark** — everyone gets an X on the arm; "we're all crew" moment. Fun ★ Effort ★

**Skypiea (Enel)** — the golden bell; the survival game.
- **Ring the golden bell** — bell hung high in a tree; hit it with a ball / Gum-Gum noodle. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Enel's survival game** — water balloon "lightning" dodge; last one dry wins. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Knock-Up Stream** — trampoline jump to grab a hanging clue. Fun ★★★ Effort ★ (needs trampoline)
- **Dial puzzle** — sound "dials" playing recorded clues (phone). Fun ★ Effort ★★

**Davy Back Fight (Foxy Pirates)** — canon pirate party games; losers give up crew members.
- **Donut Race** — three-legged or "barrel" (box) race. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Groggy Ring** — keep-away / ball-into-goal with a "ball" kid. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Combat** — pool-noodle captain duel. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- Works great as a competitive block if you split into crews. Fun ★★★ Effort ★

**Water 7 / Enies Lobby (Robin, Franky)** — Robin taken; crew declares war; Going Merry farewell.
- **Decode the Poneglyph** — Robin's cipher station with the 3D-printed decoder wheel (already designed). Fun ★★★ Effort ★★
- **Shoot down the flag** — Nerf the printed World Government flag off its post to "declare war". Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Franky's build** — build the tallest tower / a raft from provided junk in 5 minutes. Fun ★★ Effort ★★
- **Aqua Laguna** — water balloon wave. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Thriller Bark (Moria, Brook)** — shadows stolen; the skeleton musician.
- **Shadow steal / flashlight tag** — at dusk; whoever's caught in the beam loses their shadow (sits out until "freed"). Fun ★★★ Effort ★ (needs dusk)
- **Brook's song** — kids must sing/hum "Binks' Sake" (or any) to open a door. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Zombie hunt** — glow-stick hunt in the dark. Fun ★★★ Effort ★

**Sabaody / Amazon Lily** — Kuma scatters the crew.
- **Scattered crew** — kids sent to separate spots and must find each other by clues. Fun ★★ Effort ★★
- **Bubble stroll** — bubble machine + soap-bubble catching. Fun ★ Effort ★

**Impel Down** — the great prison break.
- **Laser maze** — yarn/crepe-paper "lasers" in a hallway; crawl through without touching. Fun ★★★ Effort ★★
- **Level by level** — mini escape room: 4–6 lock/riddle steps. Fun ★★★ Effort ★★★

**Marineford** — the Summit War; Ace.
- **Rescue Ace** — Nerf/water-balloon battle vs. adult "Marines"; get the hostage (stuffed animal or an older cousin) back to the ship. Fun ★★★ Effort ★★
- **Capture the flag** variant with Marine/Pirate badges (3D-printable). Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Whitebeard's quake** — everyone hides; host bangs a drum. Fun ★ Effort ★

### Timeskip
- **Two years pass** — pizza / cake break. Return with Haki wristbands (a bonus in later stations). Fun ★★ Effort ★

### Grand Line — New World

**Fishman Island** — undersea.
- **Bubble coating** — walk with a balloon between knees, or a "hold your breath while…" challenge. Fun ★★ Effort ★
- **Sea Kings** — sponge/water dodge. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Punk Hazard** — half fire, half ice.
- **Hot & cold** — hunt with "hotter/colder" hints; ice-cube-holding endurance. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Dressrosa (Doflamingo)** — strings; the Colosseum; toys.
- **Doflamingo's strings** — the yarn laser maze belongs here too. Fun ★★★ Effort ★★
- **Colosseum bracket** — mini tournament of any physical duel (noodle swords, thumb war). Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Turned to toys** — freeze tag; "toys" can't move until touched by the birthday kid. Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Zou / Whole Cake Island (Big Mom)** — the cake!
- **Bake/steal Big Mom's cake** — the birthday cake IS the wedding cake; the crew must "steal" it past a guard. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Homies** — talking-object hunt (host does voices). Fun ★★ Effort ★

**Wano (Kaido)** — Onigashima raid.
- **Kaido piñata** — the piñata you already own, as the final boss. Pool-noodle sword, blindfold = Observation Haki, bounty turn order, birthday kid closes with a Conqueror's Haki extra swing. "Kaido enters Dragon Form — everyone attack!" if it's stubborn. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Key inside** — the padlock key to the treasure chest is in the piñata → can't open the One Piece until Kaido falls. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **Ninja/samurai course** — obstacle course to Onigashima. Fun ★★ Effort ★★

**Egghead / Laugh Tale**
- **The Road Poneglyph reading** — assemble the 4 pieces, set the wheel to 13, decode the location. Fun ★★★ Effort ★★ (printing)
- **The One Piece chest** — spray-painted chest, chocolate coins, favors, cake or the gift. Roger's note inside. Fun ★★★ Effort ★
- **"The One Piece is real"** — border glyphs on the slab decode to this. Easter egg. Fun ★ Effort — (already done)

## Part 3 — Endings (pick one)

| Ending | Beat | Fun | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Locked chest + Kaido key** | Poneglyphs → chest location; chest is padlocked; key is inside the piñata; beat Kaido → open the One Piece. Two payoffs. | ★★★ | ★★ |
| **B. Piñata IS the One Piece** | Simplest: the treasure is inside the piñata. | ★★ | ★ |
| **C. Enies Lobby** | Nerf war to shoot down the World Government flag; cake appears when it falls. | ★★★ | ★ |
| **D. Onigashima raid** | Obstacle course → Kaido piñata; no chest. | ★★ | ★ |
| **E. Marineford** | Big war as the finale; rescuing "Ace" reveals the cake. | ★★★ | ★★ |
| **F. Whole Cake** | Steal the birthday cake from "Big Mom". Ends on cake, literally. | ★★★ | ★ |

## Part 6 — One suggested lineup (just a pick from the menu)
1. Romance Dawn — Devil Fruit hunt
2. Assemble the crew — Usopp / Sanji / Zoro gauntlet
3. Grand Line — Log Pose navigation → 4. Alabasta sand-key dig
5. Enies Lobby — decoder wheel cipher
6. Marineford — rescue Ace
Finale — assemble Poneglyphs → locked chest → Kaido piñata → key → One Piece.
~90 min + food. Swap in Skypiea bell, Davy Back Fight, or Thriller Bark flashlight
tag depending on yard, daylight, and headcount.
