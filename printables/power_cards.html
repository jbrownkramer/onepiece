Devil Fruit power cards — print, cut, roll up inside each hidden Devil Fruit. One-time use unless noted.

Gum-Gum Fruit
Gomu Gomu no Mi
Monkey D. Luffy
Stretch: take one extra swing, shot, or step in any challenge.

vs. Kaido: swing without the blindfold.
Rubber body. Hates swimming.
Flame-Flame Fruit
Mera Mera no Mi
Portgas D. Ace / Sabo
Fire Fist: burn away one obstacle — skip one part of a challenge for your whole crew.
Do not actually set anything on fire.
Chop-Chop Fruit
Bara Bara no Mi
Buggy the Clown
Split: the crew may divide into two search parties for one station.

vs. Kaido: two swings.
Immune to slashing. Not to being made fun of.
Op-Op Fruit
Ope Ope no Mi
Trafalgar Law
Room — Shambles: swap places with any other crew member in the turn order, once.
The Ultimate Devil Fruit. Allegedly.
Clear-Clear Fruit
Suke Suke no Mi
Absalom / Shiryu
Invisible: during the Marineford war, you may not be shot for 30 seconds. Announce it loudly.
You are still visible. Just pretend.
Sand-Sand Fruit
Suna Suna no Mi
Crocodile
Desert Spada: at Alabasta, you may use both hands and a scoop instead of one hand.
Weak to water. Bring a spray bottle.
Rumble-Rumble Fruit
Goro Goro no Mi
Enel
El Thor: the host must reveal one hint about the next station.
Do not use near rubber people.
Human-Human Fruit
Hito Hito no Mi
Tony Tony Chopper
Doctor: retry any failed challenge for the whole crew, once.

Also: you get a snack.
Cotton candy preferred.
Soul-Soul Fruit
Soru Soru no Mi
Big Mom
Life or Treat: demand one power card from another crew member. They must hand it over.
Use wisely. Friendships are at stake.
Paw-Paw Fruit
Nikyu Nikyu no Mi
Bartholomew Kuma
Repel: in the war, one hit that would tag you is repelled. Say "PAW" when it happens.
Where would you like to go on vacation?
Shadow-Shadow Fruit
Kage Kage no Mi
Gecko Moria
Shadow Steal: at any station, take 15 seconds off the clock — or add 15 seconds if you're the one racing.
Works best at dusk.
Straw Hat (no fruit)
Just a really nice hat
Given by Shanks
Captain's Word: pick which crew member goes first at any station.
Give it back when you're a great pirate.
